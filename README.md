# Enhanced Utility Pouches

Pouches and bags for everything that isn't medical: a utility pouch, an ammo pouch, an ammo bag,
an engineer bag and a CBRN bag. Carry one, fill it with what it's made for, open it like a pouch.

Runs on its own, or next to [Enhanced First Aid
Kits](https://github.com/MissHeda/Enhanced-First-Aid-Kits): it brings EFAK's kit framework along, and
with both loaded the pouches and the first aid kits share it - one arsenal tab, one kit window. Needs
[CBA_A3](https://github.com/CBATeam/CBA_A3) and [ACE3](https://github.com/acemod/ACE3), on the server
and every client.

## The pouches

| Pouch | Takes | Holds | Packed size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Utility Pouch** | anything that fits - chemlights, smokes, cable ties, a map | 30 | 12 |
| **Ammo Pouch** | magazines and grenades | 50 | 20 |
| **Ammo Bag** | magazines, belts, grenades, rockets | 240 | 95 |
| **Engineer Bag** | tools and explosives - toolkit, wire cutter, defusal kit, charges, mines | 200 | 80 |
| **CBRN Bag** | CBRN gear - suit, respirator, breathing set, detector watch, filters, decon kit (vanilla, KAT, ACM) | 250 | 100 |

What a pouch holds is a CBA setting; the room it takes in a uniform, vest or backpack is fixed. Its
weight is the empty pouch plus what is inside.

## Features

Everything Enhanced First Aid Kits does for its kits, the pouches do too:

- **Pouch window** - your gear on the left, the pouch on the right; double click, drag, **Ctrl** or **Shift** click.
- **Quick access** - see what's inside and take a stack out without opening the pouch.
- **Own menu** - the pouches have their own "Pouches" entry in the interaction menu, apart from the first aid kits.
- **ACE Arsenal** - fill every pouch on its own; loadouts keep what's inside.
- **Eden crates** - set what the pouches in a crate or vehicle hold, in its Eden attributes.
- **Mission settings** - capacity, default contents, what may go in, weight - in their own CBA settings category.
- **Own models** - every pouch has its own model when dropped.
